Was das Spiel auf deinem Gerät speichert

Im lokalen Speicher des Browsers bzw. der App (localStorage) liegen:

  • die Bestenlisten: das Kürzel aus drei Buchstaben, das du selbst eingibst, die Punktzahl, die Zahl der Aufträge, das Datum und der Startcode (Seed) des Spiels,
  • deine Einstellungen: Modus, Sprache, Hitze, ob die Anleitung schon gezeigt wurde,
  • der Spielstand eines laufenden Beutezugs, damit er nach einem Neustart weitergeht,
  • in der App: ob die Vollversion gekauft ist.

Diese Daten verlassen dein Gerät nie; wir können sie nicht einsehen. Du löschst sie, indem du die Browserdaten der Seite bzw. in Android die App-Daten löschst oder die App deinstallierst.

Verantwortlich für die Verarbeitung im Rahmen von Google Play ist Google; es gilt die Datenschutzerklärung von Google. What the game stores on your device

The browser's or app's local storage (localStorage) holds:

  • the high scores: the three-letter initials you enter yourself, the score, the number of jobs, the date and the game's starting code (seed),
  • your settings: mode, language, heat, whether the tutorial has been shown,
  • the save of a heist in progress, so it can continue after a restart,
  • in the app: whether the full version has been bought.

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You delete it by clearing the site's browser data, or the app's data in Android, or by uninstalling the app.

Buying the full version (Android app)

The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (Google Play Billing). You enter payment details only with Google; we do not receive them. The app only asks Google Play whether the full version has been bought for your Google account. For accounting, Google provides us with order details (such as order number, product, price and country). Google is responsible for processing within Google Play; the Google Privacy Policy applies. Our legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Closed test of the Android app

If you want to test the app before its release, you join the Google group beutezug-tester yourself (Google Groups) and then accept the test on Google Play. The website and the game transmit nothing in the process; both steps take place at Google, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. As the group's managers we see the email address of your Google account. We use it only to open the test to you and, if needed, to write to you about the test through the group. The legal basis is your consent by joining (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can leave the group at any time, which also ends the test for you. Once the test is over, we delete the group.

This website

The website and the browser version of the game are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, for example to defend against attacks; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site sets no cookies, embeds no third-party services and loads no fonts or scripts from other servers. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in operating the site securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The same demo is also available on itch.io. There, itch.io runs the page around the game and the itch.io privacy policy applies; the game itself behaves exactly as it does here.

Children

The game has a kids mode. There, too, no data is collected or transmitted, there are no ads and no external links. The full version can only be bought through Google Play's purchase dialog; kids mode says that a grown-up is needed for it.

Selected unwanted letter sequences on the game board are checked entirely on your device using a built-in list. No input is sent to a service for this check.
